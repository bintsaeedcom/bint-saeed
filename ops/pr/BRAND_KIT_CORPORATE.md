# Bint Saeed — Corporate PR Brand Kit (locked)

**Angle:** professional house / corporate communications.  
**Forbidden:** founder biography, personal origin story, family narrative, “I founded…”.  
**Source of truth:** live site copy in this repo + `https://www.bintsaeed.com` — never invent claims.

## House line (approved)

> A contemporary fashion house from Abu Dhabi devoted to evolving lifestyles.

## Positioning (claim-safe)

- Contemporary fashion house based in **Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates**
- Design language: **six House Codes** drawn from Emirati craft tradition, translated into contemporary form
- Motto direction: **Carrying Heritage Forward** — heritage continues through contemporary design (not costume nostalgia)
- Primarily **made to order**; patterns developed in **Italy**; production directed in **Abu Dhabi**
- Materials sourced from Europe & Asia; controlled volumes
- Craftsmen directing production with **25+ years’** experience (as stated on craftsmanship pages)
- Audience: contemporary women seeking refined dressing across cities, roles, and borders

## Named entities (preserve exactly)

Bint Saeed · House Codes / The Codes · The Monogram · Al Talli · Al Khous · Al Ain Rosette (Rosette d’Al Ain / روزيت العين) · Knotted Lines · The Strands · Al Quaa · Al Ain Oasis · Abu Dhabi · United Arab Emirates · Emirati heritage

## Product pillars for outreach

1. Ready-to-wear — abayas, kaftans, dresses, sets  
2. The Strands — natural-stone strands as wearable house code  
3. Jewellery & accessories — Al Ain Oasis / Al Quaa lines; bag charms; phone charms  
4. House Codes as cultural-design story (editorial)  
5. Giving Forward — **AED 20 from each piece** dedicated (inshallah) to named UAE charity partners on site (Mother of the Nation Endowment for Orphans; Emirates Red Crescent initiatives)

## Never invent

- Founder names/bios, CD personal life, “first/only Emirati luxury house”
- That every garment uses every craft code
- Stone origins, purity, “ethical mining” unless on that PDP
- Charity amounts other than AED 20 / piece or partners not named on site
- Shipping promises beyond published policy
- Dubai-as-HQ (house is Abu Dhabi)
