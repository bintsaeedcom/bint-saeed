# Pitch templates (corporate voice)

All drafts: calm, precise, short. No hype, no emoji, no “thrilled to connect”, no founder story.  
Personalise the **first sentence** with a real proof link from research.  
Max ~120 words for cold email; ~80 for IG/LinkedIn short form.

## Variables

- `{{person_or_desk}}`
- `{{org}}`
- `{{proof_line}}` — one concrete observation from their work
- `{{angle_hook}}` — from brand kit angle
- `{{primary_url}}` — usually /about or /the-codes
- `{{cta}}` — lookbook PDF / press images / sample conversation / listing update

## Replies & follow-up (inbound)

Outbound PR pitches are **not** sent through Resend today. After you approve, you send from the house mailbox (Google Workspace / domain email as Bint Saeed Communications).

**Resend** on this site is for shopper transactional mail (orders, gift cards, subscribe thank-yous) — not PR outreach.

**When people reply or contact the house back:**
1. Route the shared inbox (e.g. press@ / hello@) so replies notify the team (Slack email integration or mailbox rules → your PR Slack channel).
2. Paste the reply into chat or `ops/pr/queue/REPLIES.md` and say `FOLLOW UP <draft-id>`.
3. The agent drafts a reply from the brand kit; you approve again before send.
4. Mark prospect status `replied` → then `nurture` or next action in the ledger.
