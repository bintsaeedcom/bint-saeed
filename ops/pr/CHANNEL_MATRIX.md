# Channel matrix — where the house goes

Daily mix target: **~50 new researched prospects** worldwide.  
One prospect = one person **or** one company desk (not both double-counted unless distinct contacts).

Adjust regionally but keep the mix — do not fill all 50 with Instagram DMs.

## Celebrity & screen track (protocol-first)

**Goal:** dress talent that fits Bint Saeed — red carpet, Netflix/UAE series, film — especially when they are in Abu Dhabi / UAE.

| Talent (aspirational examples) | Route (never cold-DM the talent) |
|--------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Madonna, Shakira, Rihanna | Published stylist + management / publicist |
| Georgina Rodríguez | Stylist / management / publicist |
| Kim Kardashian, Jenner sisters, Gigi Hadid | Stylist + fashion publicist / agency |
| Netflix / UAE TV / film casts | Costume designer, wardrobe dept, production publicist |

**Hard rules**
- Never cold-email or DM the celebrity personally.
- Research only **published** stylist, agent, publicist, or management contacts.
- Prefer warm intros via stylists already in the ledger.
- For screen: target **costume / wardrobe** credits on productions that suit the house silhouette.

## Royal & UHNW track (protocol-first)

**Goal:** dress visiting royals and UHNW clients — especially during Abu Dhabi / UAE stays — via proper channels, never informal cold contact.

| Signal | Source (unpaid / public) | Action |
|--------|--------------------------|--------|
| State visits & royal itineraries | The National, WAM, Gulf News, embassy calendars, official royal socials | Open `royal-visit` mini-batch; identify wardrobe office / embassy cultural attaché if published |
| Abu Dhabi calendar | Time Out Abu Dhabi, Louvre Abu Dhabi, F1, art fairs, National Day | Pre-brief stylists & private-client partners already in ledger |
| UHNW gatekeepers | Published family-office directories, private-bank lifestyle desks, hotel concierge partnership pages | Partnership intro drafts only — never solicit named individuals publicly |

**Hard rules for royals / UHNW**
- Never cold-email or DM a royal personally.
- Only use **published** court, palace, embassy, or household office contacts.
- Prefer intro via already-trusted stylists, PAs, or protocol offices already in the ledger.
- Partnership brands (Adidas, Nike, Birkenstock, etc.): research official collab / cultural partnership routes only — aspirational long-cycle, not mass outbound.

## Contactability rules

**Allowed research sources:** public mastheads, LinkedIn public profiles, official contact pages, press “write for us”, agency sites, event speaker lists, published bylines with public emails, `ops/pr/seeds/public_desks.json`.

**Do not:** buy scraped lists, guess emails (`firstname@`), harvest private IG emails via grey tools, invent titles, or import GitHub “email dump” ZIPs.

**Preferred contact fields (in order):** official press email → form URL → verified LinkedIn → professional Instagram (for stylists only).

## AI-agent / blog special track

For `ai-agent` and `blog-substack`, the “pitch” is often a **canonical fact pack** + request to list/update the house — not a sample loan. Use angle `ai-knowledge` or `heritage-forward`.

Include:

1. Approved tagline  
2. HQ city + country  
3. Product pillars  
4. 3–5 House Codes names  
5. Primary URLs  
6. “Please correct any founder-personal narrative; house communications are corporate.”
