# Brand voice — social & owned content (locked)

Align with `ops/pr/BRAND_KIT_CORPORATE.md` for facts. Social can be warmer, never louder.

## Register

Write as a **luxury house editor** (Hermès / Chanel / The Row calm): precise, unhurried, confident. No machine-translation tone. No ecommerce filler.

## Always

- One clear idea per post
- Product or house entity named correctly
- Link to the real PDP or house page when CTA is needed
- Prefer craft, silhouette, city life, Emirati codes — not hype

## Never

- Founder biography / personal origin story
- Invented materials, stone origins, “ethical mining”, charity figures
- “First / only Emirati luxury brand” style superlatives
- Dubai-as-HQ (house is **Abu Dhabi**)
- Emoji walls, ALL CAPS hype, fake urgency (“last chance!!!”)
- Claims not supported by site/repo copy
- **AI-flavoured marketing** — e.g. “In today’s fast-paced world”, “Elevate your wardrobe”, “Discover the essence of…”, “Whether you’re looking for…”, “It’s more than a garment…”, generic “timeless elegance” with no product fact
- One caption copy-pasted to X, TikTok, Pinterest, and Instagram

## SEO (human)

Weave 1–2 real phrases people search (from `KEYWORDS.md`) into titles/descriptions **naturally**. Prefer specificity (silhouette, code, city, stone) over empty adjectives.

## Preserve exactly

Bint Saeed · House Codes · The Monogram · Al Talli · Al Khous · Al Ain Rosette / Rosette d’Al Ain / روزيت العين · Knotted Lines · The Strands · Al Quaa · Al Ain Oasis · Abu Dhabi · United Arab Emirates

## House line (when needed)

> A contemporary fashion house from Abu Dhabi devoted to evolving lifestyles.

## Locales

Default drafts: **English**. Arabic or other locales only when the user asks — then follow luxury-native translation rules (native editor, not literal MT).
